Getting started

From "let's do it" to a live website.

Exactly what happens, what you pay and when, and what I'll need from you. Your part takes about an hour in total.

Say yes & pay the deposit

Reply "let's do it" and tell me where to send the invoice. Pay the $375 deposit online through Square. That reserves your founding spot.

Day 1

Send your info

Answer a short questionnaire and send photos of your work by email or text. Happy to go through it on a quick call instead.

About 10 minutes

Review your draft

About a week later, look at it on your phone and send all your changes in one message. Two rounds of changes are included.

About a week

Approve, pay & launch

Approve it in writing, pay the final $375, and I connect your domain, test the quote form with you and link it from your Google profile.

Day ~14
What you pay
  • $375 deposit to start
  • $375 when you approve the finished site, before it goes live
  • Your domain, about $12–20 a year, paid to the company you buy it from
  • No monthly fee required. Hosting is included
What you get
  • Up to 5 pages, written for you
  • Built for phones, fast and secure
  • Call buttons and a quote form that emails you
  • Google basics and a review button
  • 2 rounds of changes and 14 days of fixes
  • You own your domain and your site

Have these handy

What I'll need from you.

With these ready, the questionnaire takes about 10 minutes. Anything you're not sure about, we figure out together.

🏷️

Business basics

Business name, the number customers should call, the email for quote requests, the areas you serve, years in business and your hours.

🧰

Your services

Your top 3–5 services in a sentence each, the job you want more of, and anything you don't do.

📷

Photos & logo

6–15 photos of your work (phone photos are fine, only ones you took or can use), plus your logo if you have one.

⭐

Reviews & licensing

3–5 reviews you love from Google, Nextdoor, Facebook or Yelp, and license or insurance details if you want them shown.
