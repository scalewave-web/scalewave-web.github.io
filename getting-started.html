Getting started

From "let's do it" to a live website

Here's exactly what happens, what you pay and when, and what I'll need from you. Your part takes about an hour in total.

The steps

  1. Say yes. Reply "let's do it" to your proposal and tell me the email you'd like the invoice sent to.
  2. Pay the $375 deposit. You'll get a Square invoice you can pay online. That reserves your founding spot and starts the work.
  3. Send your info (about 10 minutes). Answer the short questionnaire and send photos of your work by email or text. Happy to go through it on a quick call instead.
  4. Buy your domain (5 minutes), if you don't have one. It's in your name, about $12–20 a year. I'll tell you exactly what to click.
  5. Review your first draft (about a week later). Look at it on your phone and send all your changes in one message.
  6. Two rounds of changes. When you're happy, tell me in writing that it's approved.
  7. Pay the final $375. Same kind of Square invoice. It's due once you approve the site, before it goes live.
  8. Launch. I connect your domain, test the quote form with you, and link the site from your Google Business Profile.
  9. 14 days of fixes. Anything that needs adjusting after launch is on me.

What you pay

  • $375 deposit to start
  • $375 when you approve the finished site, before it goes live
  • Your domain, about $12–20 a year, paid directly to the company you buy it from
  • No monthly fee required. Hosting is included

What you get

  • Up to 5 pages, written for you
  • Built for phones, fast and secure
  • Call buttons and a quote form that emails you
  • Google basics and a review button
  • 2 rounds of changes and 14 days of fixes
  • You own your domain and your site

What I'll need from you

Have these handy and the questionnaire takes about 10 minutes. Anything you're not sure about, we can figure out together.

Business basics

Business name, the phone number customers should call, the email where quote requests should go, your city and the areas you serve, years in business and your hours.

Your services

Your top 3–5 services in a sentence each, the job you want more of, and anything you don't do.

Photos and logo

6–15 photos of your work, before/afters, your truck or team (phone photos are fine, and only ones you took or can use), plus your logo if you have one.

Reviews and licensing

3–5 reviews you love from Google, Nextdoor, Facebook or Yelp, and your license or insurance details if you want them shown.
