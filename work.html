What a ScaleWave site looks like

Two concept sites for fictional West Valley businesses, built with the same system a client site uses. They're labeled as samples on every page. The businesses, phone numbers and reviews are made up; the design and features are exactly what you'd get.

Landscaping concept · fictional business

Saguaro Ridge Landscaping

A site for a low-water landscaping company in Goodyear: desert landscape design, irrigation repair and weekly maintenance across Goodyear, Avondale, Buckeye, Litchfield Park and Tolleson.

What was built

  • 6 pages: Home, Services, About, Reviews & Work, Contact and a thank-you page
  • A headline that says what they do and where, with "Get a free quote" and "Call" buttons right at the top
  • A "Call now" button that stays on screen while scrolling on phones
  • A quote form with a service dropdown, so the owner knows what each request is about
  • Service details with what's included, plus the cities they serve
  • Reviews and a photo gallery section (sample reviews are labeled as samples)
  • Google basics: page titles and descriptions, landscaping business info for search engines, sitemap

House cleaning concept · fictional business

Sonoran Sparkle Cleaning

A site for a house cleaning team in Avondale offering recurring, deep and move-in/move-out cleaning in Avondale, Goodyear, Litchfield Park and Tolleson.

  • The same 6-page layout, in the cleaning company's own colors
  • Clear service packages (recurring, deep, move-in/move-out) with what each one covers
  • Trust points front and center: a friendly, background-checked local team
