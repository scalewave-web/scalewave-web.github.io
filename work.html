Landscaping concept · fictional business

Saguaro Ridge Landscaping

A site for a low-water landscaping company in Goodyear: desert landscape design, irrigation repair and weekly maintenance across Goodyear, Avondale, Buckeye, Litchfield Park and Tolleson.

  • 6 pages: Home, Services, About, Reviews & Work, Contact and a thank-you page
  • A headline that says what they do and where, with quote and call buttons right at the top
  • A "Call now" button that stays on screen while scrolling on phones
  • A quote form with a service dropdown, so the owner knows what each request is about
  • Service details, the cities they serve, reviews and a photo gallery (sample reviews labeled)
  • Google basics: page titles, descriptions, landscaping business info, sitemap
