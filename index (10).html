How it works

About two weeks from start to launch. Your part takes roughly an hour in total.

  1. Quick call (10 minutes). We talk about your services, the areas you serve and the jobs you want more of.
  2. Proposal and deposit. You get a one-screen proposal. Reply "let's do it" and pay the $375 deposit through a Square invoice.
  3. Questionnaire and photos (about 10 minutes). Answer a few questions and send me photos of your work (email or text). Phone photos are fine.
  4. First draft in about a week. You review it on your phone and send all your changes in one message.
  5. Two rounds of changes. I make your edits until you're happy, then you approve the finished site.
  6. Final $375, then launch. The site goes live on your own domain, the quote form is tested, and it's linked from your Google profile.
  7. 14 days of fixes. Anything that needs tweaking after launch is on me.
