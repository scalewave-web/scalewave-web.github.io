FAQ

Questions owners ask

Are there monthly fees?

No monthly fee is required. Hosting is included; your only ongoing cost is your domain, about $12–20 a year, in your name.

Have you built sites for other businesses?

You'd be one of my first three clients, which is why the founding price is $750. The sample sites show the quality you'd get, and you approve the design before the second payment.

Will this get me on the first page of Google?

Nobody honest can promise rankings. I set your site up the way Google expects and link it from your Google Business Profile, so people searching for you find a real website.

What do I need to provide?

About 10 minutes of answers (services, areas, hours) and some photos of your work. I write all the text from there.

Can I update the site myself?

Just send me your changes. Fixes are free for the first 14 days after launch; after that I quote any change before doing it.

What if I don't like it?

You review the draft and get two rounds of changes. The second half is only due once you approve the finished site, right before it goes live.

How do I pay?

I send an invoice through Square that you can pay online: $375 to start and $375 when you approve the finished site.

Do you use AI?

I use AI tools to help me write and build faster, which is part of how the price stays at $750. Everything is tailored to your business, and you approve every word and photo.

Can I get more than 5 pages?

Yes. Extra pages are $150 each, for example a page for each city you serve or a seasonal service. Most owners start with 5 and add later.

I already have a Facebook page. Do I need a website?

Facebook is great for updates, but people searching Google for your service in your city mostly click websites. A site works alongside your Facebook page.

Are you local?

Yes. I'm Dylan, based in the Phoenix West Valley, and I work with businesses in Goodyear, Avondale, Buckeye, Litchfield Park, Glendale, Peoria, Surprise and nearby cities.
